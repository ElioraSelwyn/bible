Psalm 117

Extol Him, All You Peoples

1Praise the LORD, all you nations!
Extol Him, all you peoples!a
2For great is His loving devotion toward us,
and the faithfulness of the LORD endures forever.
Hallelujah!b
Translation footnotes (2)
  1. 117:1 Cited in Romans 15:11
  2. 117:2 Or Hallelu YAH, meaning Praise the LORD
